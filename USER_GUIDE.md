## Introduction

### What is Chai?

Chai is an omnichannel AI-powered customer operations platform designed to help businesses manage customer interactions across multiple channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, website chat, etc.) from a single unified interface.

### Key Benefits

- **Unified Inbox**: Manage all customer conversations from one place
- **AI-Powered Automation**: Let AI handle routine inquiries automatically
- **Multi-Channel Support**: Connect with customers wherever they are
- **Team Collaboration**: Assign conversations, add internal notes, and work together
- **Analytics & Insights**: Track performance and customer satisfaction
- **Payment Integration**: Process payments directly from conversations
- **Shipment Tracking**: Monitor deliveries and logistics

### Who is this for?

- Customer support teams
- Sales teams
- Operations managers
- Business owners
- Customer success managers

## Getting Started

### First Login

1. Open your browser and navigate to your Chai instance URL
2. Enter your credentials (email and password)
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Initial Setup

#### 1. Configure Your Channels

Before you can receive messages, you need to connect your communication channels:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Channels**
2. Click "Add Channel"
3. Select your channel type (WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, etc.)
4. Follow the authentication steps for each channel
5. Test the connection

#### 2. Set Up Your Team

1. Go to **Settings** → **Team**
2. Click "Invite Team Member"
3. Enter their email address
4. Assign a role (Admin, Agent, Manager)
5. Click "Send Invitation"

#### 3. Configure AI Agent (Optional)

1. Go to **AI Agent** → **Configuration**
2. Enable AI assistance
3. Set your business rules and tone
4. Define when AI should hand over to humans
5. Test with sample conversations

## Main Features

### 1. Unified Inbox

The Unified Inbox is your command center for all customer conversations.

**What you can do:**
- View all conversations from all channels in one place
- Filter by status (Open, Pending, Resolved)
- Search conversations by customer name, message content, or tags
- Assign conversations to team members
- Add internal notes (not visible to customers)
- Send messages, images, files, and quick replies
- Mark conversations as resolved

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Inbox** in the left sidebar
2. Select a conversation from the list
3. Read the conversation history
4. Type your reply in the message box at the bottom
5. Click "Send" or press Enter

**Pro Tips:**
- Use keyboard shortcuts: `Ctrl+Enter` to send, `Ctrl+Shift+A` to assign
- Pin important conversations for quick access
- Use tags to categorize conversations (e.g., "urgent", "billing", "technical")

### 2. Contacts

Manage your customer database and build relationships.

**What you can do:**
- View all customer contacts
- Create new contacts manually
- Edit contact information
- Delete contacts
- Import contacts from CSV/Excel
- Export contacts to CSV
- Segment contacts by tags, location, or behavior
- View contact history and interactions

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Contacts** in the left sidebar
2. Use the search bar to find specific contacts
3. Click on a contact to view their profile
4. Click "Edit" to update information
5. Click "Add Tag" to categorize the contact

**Pro Tips:**
- Use bulk actions to tag or delete multiple contacts at once
- Create segments for targeted marketing campaigns
- Export contacts regularly for backup

### 3. Payments

Process payments directly from conversations or create payment links.

**What you can do:**
- Create payment links for customers
- View payment history
- Process refunds
- Track payment status (Pending, Paid, Failed)
- Generate payment reports
- Set up recurring payments

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Payments** in the left sidebar
2. Click "Create Payment Link"
3. Enter amount, currency, and description
4. Click "Generate Link"
5. Copy the link and send it to your customer

**Pro Tips:**
- Use payment links in conversations for seamless checkout
- Set up automatic payment reminders
- Track payment success rates in Analytics

### 4. Shipments

Track and manage shipments and deliveries.

**What you can do:**
- Create new shipments
- Track shipment status in real-time
- View shipment history
- Update shipment status manually
- Print shipping labels
- Notify customers about shipment updates

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Shipments** in the left sidebar
2. Click "Create Shipment"
3. Enter shipment details (origin, destination, package info)
4. Select shipping provider
5. Click "Create"
6. Share tracking link with customer

**Pro Tips:**
- Enable automatic status updates from logistics providers
- Use bulk shipment creation for multiple orders
- Set up delivery notifications

### 5. Team Management

Manage your team members, roles, and permissions.

**What you can do:**
- Invite new team members
- Assign roles (Admin, Manager, Agent)
- Set permissions for each role
- View team member activity
- Deactivate/reactivate accounts
- Monitor team performance

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Team** in the left sidebar
2. Click "Invite Member"
3. Enter email and select role
4. Click "Send Invitation"
5. Team member will receive an email to set up their account

**Pro Tips:**
- Use role-based access control to limit sensitive actions
- Monitor agent performance metrics
- Set up automated assignments based on workload

### 6. Analytics & Reports

Track performance metrics and gain insights.

**What you can do:**
- View conversation metrics (response time, resolution time)
- Track agent performance
- Monitor customer satisfaction
- Analyze channel performance
- Generate custom reports
- Export data to CSV/PDF

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Analytics** in the left sidebar
2. Select date range
3. Choose metrics to display
4. View charts and graphs
5. Click "Export" to download report

**Pro Tips:**
- Set up automated daily/weekly reports
- Compare performance across different time periods
- Use insights to optimize your customer support strategy

### 7. Knowledge Base

Create and manage help articles and FAQs.

**What you can do:**
- Create knowledge articles
- Organize articles by categories
- Search articles
- Edit and update articles
- Track article views and helpfulness
- Use articles for AI training

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Knowledge** in the left sidebar
2. Click "New Article"
3. Write your article content
4. Add tags and categories
5. Click "Publish"

**Pro Tips:**
- Use knowledge base articles to train your AI agent
- Keep articles updated and accurate
- Use clear, simple language

### 8. AI Agent Configuration

Configure your AI assistant to handle conversations automatically.

**What you can do:**
- Enable/disable AI agent
- Set business rules and tone
- Define handover conditions
- Train AI with knowledge base articles
- Monitor AI performance
- Review AI-handled conversations

**How to use:**
1. Click on **AI Agent** in the left sidebar
2. Go to **Configuration**
3. Enable AI agent
4. Set your preferences (tone, language, business rules)
5. Define when to hand over to humans
6. Save changes

**Pro Tips:**
- Start with conservative settings and gradually expand AI capabilities
- Monitor AI-handled conversations regularly
- Update knowledge base to improve AI accuracy

### 9. Automation Builder

Create automated workflows to save time.

**What you can do:**
- Create automation rules (triggers + actions)
- Set up auto-assignments
- Schedule automated messages
- Create conditional workflows
- Test automations before activating

**How to use:**
1. Click on **Automation** in the left sidebar
2. Click "New Automation"
3. Set trigger (e.g., "New conversation from WhatsApp")
4. Add actions (e.g., "Assign to Agent X", "Send welcome message")
5. Set conditions if needed
6. Click "Activate"

**Pro Tips:**
- Start with simple automations
- Test thoroughly before activating
- Monitor automation performance

## Feature Details

### Conversation Lifecycle

1. **New**: Customer sends first message
2. **Open**: Conversation is active
3. **Pending**: Waiting for customer response
4. **Resolved**: Issue resolved, conversation closed
5. **Closed**: No further action needed

### Conversation Assignment

**Manual Assignment:**
1. Open conversation
2. Click "Assign" button
3. Select team member
4. Confirm assignment

**Auto-Assignment:**
- Set up rules in Automation Builder
- Assign based on channel, tags, or customer segment
- Round-robin distribution available

### Internal Notes

Internal notes are private messages visible only to your team, not to customers.

**How to add:**
1. Open conversation
2. Click "Add Note" button
3. Type your note
4. Click "Save"

**Use cases:**
- Document customer preferences
- Share context with team members
- Track investigation steps
- Leave reminders for follow-up

### Quick Replies

Save time with pre-written responses for common questions.

**How to create:**
1. Go to **Settings** → **Quick Replies**
2. Click "New Quick Reply"
3. Enter shortcut (e.g., "/hours")
4. Enter full response
5. Click "Save"

**How to use:**
1. In conversation, type "/" to see available quick replies
2. Select one or type shortcut
3. Response is inserted automatically
4. Edit if needed, then send

### Tags

Tags help you categorize and filter conversations and contacts.

**Common tags:**
- `urgent` - High priority
- `billing` - Payment-related
- `technical` - Technical support
- `sales` - Sales inquiry
- `complaint` - Customer complaint
- `feedback` - Customer feedback

**How to add:**
1. Open conversation or contact
2. Click "Add Tag"
3. Select existing tag or create new one
4. Tag is saved automatically

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Issue: Buttons not working

**Solution:**
- Refresh the page (Ctrl + F5)
- Clear browser cache
- Check browser console for errors (F12)
- Try a different browser
- Contact support if issue persists

#### Issue: Messages not sending

**Solution:**
- Check internet connection
- Verify channel connection in Settings → Channels
- Check if conversation is resolved (reopen if needed)
- Refresh the page

#### Issue: Can't assign conversation

**Solution:**
- Check if you have permission to assign
- Verify team member is active
- Refresh the page
- Contact admin if issue persists

#### Issue: Analytics not loading

**Solution:**
- Check date range (not too far in past)
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Contact support if issue persists

#### Issue: AI not responding

**Solution:**
- Check if AI is enabled in AI Agent → Configuration
- Verify knowledge base has relevant articles
- Check AI handover conditions
- Review AI logs for errors

### Getting Help

If you encounter issues:

1. **Check this guide** - Most common questions are answered here
